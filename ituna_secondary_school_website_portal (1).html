About Ituna Secondary School

Founded in 1933 in Kasama, Northern Province, Zambia

Ituna Secondary School has established itself as an institution of high educational standards, dedicated to shaping young minds for academic success and practical life skills.

Located in Kasama, Northern Province, the school caters to both junior and senior secondary school students under the Ministry of General Education standards in Zambia.

Motto

"The Fountain of Knowledge"

Core Objectives

  • Provide holistic secondary education.
  • Maintain top pass rates in ECZ Grade 9 and Grade 12 examinations.
  • Foster discipline, leadership, and community service values.
